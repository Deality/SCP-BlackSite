SCP: The Blacksite

"I used to be an ordinary journalist... until I found the truth." "Sıradan bir gazeteciydim... Ta ki gerçeği bulana kadar."

A first-person horror/survival looter inspired by Lethal Company and Content Warning, set in the SCP Foundation universe.


About

You are Elias Voss, a journalist who got too close to the truth about the SCP Foundation's Site-17. Captured during an anomalous incident and imprisoned, you're forced to sign a deadly contract: retrieve anomalous entities and samples from compromised facilities in exchange for your life.

"Bring us the anomalies. In return, we'll let you live."

Every facility you enter, every SCP you catch, and every sample you deliver keeps you alive a little longer — but one mistake could be your last.

Game Info
	
Genre	FPS / Horror / Survival
Core mechanic	Looting & extraction
Mode	Single player
Platform	Windows / PC
Engine	Unity
Gameplay Loop
Prepare — Buy equipment from the MSV (Mobile Security Vehicle) terminal and pick your next facility.
Explore — Search the facility for samples and anomalous SCPs.
Extract — Carry captured SCPs and collected samples back to the MSV.
Deliver — Turn in your haul, get paid, and upgrade the MSV.
Repeat — Take on harder, higher-security facilities as your quota rises.
Features
Story-driven single player campaign following Elias Voss
Set within the SCP Foundation universe
Loot-and-escape gameplay loop with a rising quota system
Hostile SCPs with unique behaviors (SCP-173, SCP-096, SCP-106, and more)
Collectible passive SCPs and lab samples to sell for profit
MSV hub: buy gear, select facilities, and upgrade over time
Security clearance system gating access to higher-risk areas
Health, stamina, and checkpoint/respawn systems
Stealth mechanics (crouching) to avoid hostile SCPs
Controls
Key	Action
WASD	Move
Shift	Run
Space	Jump
Ctrl	Crouch
E	Interact / pick up item / catch stunned SCP
G	Drop held item
1 2 3 4	Select inventory slot
How to Run

This repository uses Git LFS for the large build files (Unity data files and the gameplay preview video). Make sure Git LFS is installed before cloning:

git lfs install
git clone https://github.com/Deality/SCP-BlackSite.git

Then simply run:

My project.exe

A gameplay preview is included as SCP_Gameplay.mp4.

Hakkında

Sen Elias Voss'sun — SCP Foundation'ın Site-17 tesisindeki gerçeğe fazla yaklaşmış bir gazeteci. Bir anomali olayı sırasında yakalanıp tutuklu düşüyorsun ve hayatta kalmak için ölümcül bir anlaşma imzalamak zorunda kalıyorsun: ele geçirilmiş tesislerden anormal varlıkları ve örnekleri getirmek.

"Anomalileri yakaladıkça, yaşamana izin vereceğiz."

Girdiğin her tesis, yakaladığın her SCP ve teslim ettiğin her örnek seni biraz daha hayatta tutar — ama tek bir hata son hatan olabilir.
